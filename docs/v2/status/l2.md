- Morning card (first four hours and no open plan) and shutdown card (near/after plan end, logged work).

- Preview selected work through L3 energy-aware scheduler; accept proposed blocks in one offline-safe W()/commit() transaction, including backlog consumption and carry-over reconciliation.
